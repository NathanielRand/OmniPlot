Here's what changed and why it works:

  Root cause: normalizePts(fitSmooth) — the σ=3.0 Gaussian applied to the full contour (circularly)
  moved corner pixel coordinates 2–3px inward, and also biased the tangent estimation at corners
  to point along the "rounded" direction. The bezier arrived at and departed from each corner along
  the rounded path.

  Fix (3-line change to traceImageData): Use normalizePts(lightSmooth) (σ=1.5) instead. Corner
  coordinates are now only ~0.5px off the true geometric corner. The multiPointTangent calls at
  span endpoints read from the σ=1.5 contour, so the leaving/arriving directions at a 90° corner
  diverge naturally — producing a cusp in the SVG path — while smooth transitions (A-pillar, top
  arc) naturally stay smooth because their tangents align.

  The sqTol of 1.5px absorbs the residual σ=1.5 staircase noise without extra segments. No per-span
  smoothing pass needed.

  Results:
  - IoU: 99.3% (↑ from 98.7%)
  - Hausdorff: 0.848 path-units (↓ from 0.867)
  - Corners: [1, 72, 311, 462] — same structural transitions
  - 63/63 tests passing
